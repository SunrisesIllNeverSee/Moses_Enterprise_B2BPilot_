Re-measurement loop

The loop is the unit of evidence.

Every intervention produces one pass through this loop. The output is a result classification — not a verdict on the operator, and not a claim about business performance.

1

BASELINE

Operator metrics from the evaluation phase. The reference for every delta.

2

INTERVENTION

Declared intervention object applied. Target metric and window recorded.

3

POST WINDOW

Telemetry collected over an equivalent span after the window closes.

4

INTERNAL METRIC DELTA

Target and non-target internal metrics compared. Both reported.

5

OPTIONAL EXTERNAL OUTCOME DELTA

If declared, external outcome joined and compared. ASSOCIATION only.

6

RESULT CLASSIFICATION

Improved / unchanged / degraded / inconclusive. About the intervention, not the person.

Result states

Seven possible outcomes. No spin.

A result is classified honestly. "Unchanged" and "inconclusive" are real outcomes — not failures to be reframed.

IMPROVED

Improved internally

Target internal metric moved in the expected direction beyond noise threshold.

UNCHANGED

Unchanged internally

Target internal metric did not move beyond noise threshold. The intervention did not produce the expected structural change.

DEGRADED

Degraded internally

Target internal metric moved against the expected direction. Worth investigating — possibly a wrong diagnosis or a harmful intervention.

EXTERNAL UP

External outcome improved

Joined external outcome moved favorably. ASSOCIATION with the internal change — not causation.

EXTERNAL FLAT

External outcome unchanged

Joined external outcome did not move. Internal improvement may not have reached the business surface — or the outcome measure was wrong.

EXTERNAL DOWN

External outcome degraded

Joined external outcome moved unfavorably. Flagged for review. Still ASSOCIATION.

INCONCLUSIVE

Inconclusive

Insufficient post-window data, noisy telemetry, or confounding changes. No classification can be made. The loop is recorded but not interpreted.

External outcome validation

Business impact is tested separately — and optionally.

External joins are optional and company-specific. They are never automatic, never default, and never treated as proof that the internal change caused the business change.

Each category is a candidate join, not a default. The company selects which outcomes to join, for which cohort, and under what governance. The join is declared in the intervention object before the window opens.

Claims discipline

What we do NOT claim.

We measure operator change directly. We test business impact separately.
NOT CLAIMED

Internal change ≠ business performance

The product must not treat internal metric change as automatic business performance change. An operator who improves context construction has improved context construction — not necessarily shipped faster.

NOT CLAIMED

Association ≠ causation

Even when an external outcome moves alongside an internal change, the relationship is association. Confounders, seasonality, and parallel initiatives are not controlled. The label is honest about this.

All outcome joins are ASSOCIATION, not CAUSATION. This is a structural claim about the method, not a disclaimer added after the fact.

Closed loop

Diagnose. Intervene. Re-measure. Classify.

The verify phase closes the loop the develop phase opened. The result classification feeds back into diagnosis — refining the hypotheses for the next pass.
