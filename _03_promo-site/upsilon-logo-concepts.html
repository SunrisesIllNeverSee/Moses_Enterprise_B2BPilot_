11. Heat Signature — Word Integrated Into Mark
11-A. Letters as cells thermal type
Letters as cells. Each letter sits inside its own heat cell. The intensity rises toward the center (I = peak) and falls off. The word IS the heatmap. The I glows because it's the measurement point. Syne font.
11-B. Grid behind type cut-through
Grid behind type. Full heat signature grid as background, dark letters cut through it. The glow shows through the counter-spaces. The word emerges from the data. Unbounded font — geometric, distinctive.
11-C. Pixel heat type dot-matrix
Pixel heat type. Each letter built from individual dots with heat intensity. The I is brightest (peak signal). The letters ARE data. Dot-matrix-meets-thermal. Completely ownable — no one else does this.
11-D. Bar-chart type data-bars
Bar-chart type. Letters constructed from vertical bars of varying height and intensity — like a data visualization. The I is the tallest, brightest bar (peak measurement). The word literally reads as a chart. Data-native typography.
L1. Crystal Lattice — Word Integrated Into Mark
L1-A. Nodes on type connected
Nodes on type. Wordmark in subtle gold, lattice connections and nodes overlaid on the letters. The 'i' dot becomes the peak node — brightest point in the lattice. The word IS the lattice structure. Syne font.
L1-B. Word as graph node-network
Word as graph. Each letter IS a lattice node-cluster connected by edges. U = U-shape, P = triangle, S = zigzag, I = vertical (peak, glowing), O = hexagon, N = zigzag. Inter-letter connections span the word. The word is a connected graph. No font needed — the lattice IS the type.
L1-C. Threaded word woven
Threaded word. Lattice grid as background, word threaded through it. The 'i' dot lands exactly on a lattice intersection — the peak node. The word lives inside the measurement framework. Unbounded font, glowing.
L1-D. Lattice U + word lockup
Lattice U + word. A U constructed from lattice nodes and connections, with the wordmark beside it. The U's base = the peak node (where measurement happens). Lockup format for headers/nav. Unbounded font.
