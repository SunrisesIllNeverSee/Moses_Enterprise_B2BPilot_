The B2B Process

From scoping call to baselines in 30 days.

Baseline → Benchmark → Diagnose → Intervene → Re-evaluate. CLI, TUI, MCP server. Cohort-level reporting. No productivity scores. No employee termination use.

Pilot Packages

Six commercial configurations. All bespoke.

Every pilot is configured around your operating reality. These are starting points, not rigid tiers. We build around your people, workflows, and AI systems.

Baseline — Starting point

Measure your operators across 5 canonical metrics. Get cohort baselines, score distributions, and percentile bands. 25–50 operators, 30 days. The foundation every other package builds on.

Diagnostic — Baseline + pattern detection

Everything in Baseline, plus divergence detection, capability gap analysis, and operator×system decomposition. Find out if the constraint is the operator, the tool, or the workflow.

Evaluation — Diagnostic + bespoke evals

Everything in Diagnostic, plus bespoke evaluation families built around your workflows and roles. Company-specific benchmarks. Internal reference populations. Workflow-level scoring.

Monitor — Evaluation + continuous tracking

Everything in Evaluation, plus ongoing measurement. Track operator performance over time. Detect drift. Re-benchmark after tool changes, training, or org restructuring.

Meta-Pilot — Monitor + intervention testing

Everything in Monitor, plus targeted intervention assignment and pre/post re-evaluation. Test whether coaching, tooling changes, or workflow redesign actually move the needle. Measure what changes.

MO§E§ — Full enterprise engagement

Everything above, plus cross-team benchmarking, organizational capability topology, outcome correlation, and executive reporting. Multi-cohort, multi-workflow, multi-system. The complete operator evaluation program.
