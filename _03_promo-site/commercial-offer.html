Privacy by Design

Content-free telemetry. No employee scoring.

The Baseline Assessment measures the structure of the operating relationship — not prompt text, not output content, not personal communications. Built for organizational measurement and development. Not employee productivity scoring. Not surveillance.

No prompt content

We never store or analyze prompt text, completions, or personal communications. Only structural telemetry.

Cohort-level by default

Reporting stays at the cohort and team level. Individual profiles are for development, not employment decisions.

Enterprise measurement

Not keystroke tracking. Not browser monitoring. Not employee productivity scoring.
