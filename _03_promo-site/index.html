Privacy by Design

Content-free telemetry. No employee scoring.

MO§ES™ measures the structure of the operating relationship — not prompt text, not output content, not personal communications. Token telemetry captures how the operator and system interact, not what they say to each other.

No prompt content

We never store or analyze prompt text, completions, or personal communications. Only structural telemetry — token counts, timing, iteration patterns.

Cohort-level by default

Reporting stays at the cohort and team level. Individual operator profiles are available for development, not for employment decisions.

Enterprise measurement, not surveillance

Built for organizational measurement and development. Not employee productivity scoring. Not keystroke tracking. Not browser monitoring.
