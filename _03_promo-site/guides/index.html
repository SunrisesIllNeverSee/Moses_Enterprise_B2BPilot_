The evaluation pipeline

How it works.

Every guide follows the same 7-phase pipeline. You don't need all seven phases to get value — even just phases 1-3 (define, instrument, compute) will tell you more about your AI operators than any usage dashboard.

PHASE 1
Define Performance

What does "good AI operator performance" mean for your team? Choose which canonical metrics matter for your business question.

PHASE 2
Collect Telemetry

Four token numbers per session: input, output, cache read, cache write. No prompt content. No output text. No PII.

PHASE 3
Compute Metrics

Five canonical derived metrics: Yield, Leverage, Token SNR, Construction, and composite score. Computed from the four primitives.

PHASE 4
Benchmark

Compare operators against cohort medians, reference percentiles, and 13 benchmark classes. Context-adjusted for task difficulty.

PHASE 5
Diagnose

Identify usage-operation divergence, workflow stage fit, capability concentration, and dependency risk. Developmental, not punitive.

PHASE 6
Intervene

Assign targeted interventions: coaching, training, workflow redesign, tool changes. Track pre/post metric changes.

PHASE 7
Track

Re-measure after intervention. Compare target vs non-target metrics. Label as ASSOCIATION, not causation, unless controlled experiment.
