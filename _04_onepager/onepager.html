Choose from 12 commercial pilots by outcome, or build your own from 15 evaluation families. The configurator validates compatibility, enforces governance rules, and produces a saveable configuration with embedded decision-use labels. Available via CLI, TUI, and MCP.
Measurement Boundary

MO§ES™ measures AI operating behavior and cohort structure. It does not claim that a higher score means a better employee, higher job performance, greater productivity, or better business outcomes without separate validation.

Privacy

The baseline can operate from telemetry without making prompt-content surveillance the default. Executive reporting can remain cohort-level. All configurations carry governance metadata: synthetic flag, decision-use labels, ASSOCIATION semantics for outcome joins, HYPOTHESIS semantics for diagnoses.
